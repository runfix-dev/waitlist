the safety net

Ship with AI. runfix catches what it breaks.

One place that guards every change, fixes what fails and protects your money and your data. Built for founders, explained in plain English.

before it ships

Every change checked

Every push, from Lovable's sync, Cursor, Claude Code or you, is built and tested in a sandbox. You get a plain verdict on GitHub: safe, risky or blocked. Turn on the required check (one GitHub setting, runfix shows the steps) and a blocked push can't merge.

when it breaks

Fixed, with proof

runfix reproduces the error, proves the fix works in a sandbox, and sends you a pull request to approve. It never merges on its own.

so it stays fixed

Fixes that stay fixed

Each fix teaches your AI tools a rule, in AGENTS.md and .cursorrules. If a later change brings the bug back, runfix flags it as blocked and tells you.

after every deploy

Bad deploy? One tap back

runfix compares errors and speed before and after each deploy. If it got worse, switch back to the last good version in one tap.

your money

questions

Fair questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. runfix explains every problem and every fix in plain English. You read it and say yes or no.

Will runfix change my app without asking?

No. Every change waits for your yes, and every change can be undone. runfix never merges code or deploys on its own.

Does it work with Lovable, Bolt or Replit?

Yes. If your app syncs to GitHub, runfix checks every change and flags risky ones on GitHub. If you publish straight from the builder, runfix can't stop a change in advance, but it catches problems right after and lets you switch back in one tap. We'll tell you which setup protects you best.

What will it cost?

Free covers one app and includes $10 of usage to try proven fixes. Founder is $19 per app a month (later $29) and Agency is $79 a month for up to 10 apps (later $129). Founding members keep their price. Extra proven fixes are $5, charged only when the fix passed every check and its pull request opened. Billing opens before launch.

Is my data safe with runfix?

runfix asks for the least access it needs, never puts your secrets into an AI prompt, and records every action it takes. Security checks are read-only.
